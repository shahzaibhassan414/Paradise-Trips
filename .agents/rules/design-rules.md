# Design Rules for Khan e Azam Travel & Tours

## Typography & Headers
1. **NO EYEBROWS / NO PRE-HEADER BADGES**:
   - Never add eyebrows, kicker badges, overline pill text, or super-headers (e.g. `<div className="...rounded-full">Curated Experiences</div>`) above `<h1>`, `<h2>`, or `<h3>` headings.
   - Headings must start directly and cleanly.
2. **NO PRICING DISPLAY**:
   - All tour packages and services are custom-quoted directly on WhatsApp. Never display `PKR`, prices, starting rates, or price sorting filters.
3. **BRAND COLOR PALETTE**:
   - Strictly Clean Emerald (`#065F46`, `#047857`, `#10B981`) & Obsidian Slate (`#0F172A`, `#020617`, `#1E293B`) on crisp light slate/white backgrounds (`#F8FAFC`).
   - Never add muddy gold, amber, or yellow tones.
4. **OFFICIAL CONTACT & SINGLE HEAD OFFICE**:
   - Sole office: `880N Poonch Rd, Samanabad Town, Lahore, 54000, Pakistan` (No Islamabad office).
   - Phone / WhatsApp: `+92 320 4127966`.
